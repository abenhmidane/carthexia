Neon Rot

Turn-based zombie puzzle · iPhone

  • Hand-built rooftop puzzles, each solvable to a par
  • Crates, transit pads, turrets, ice, patrols and the breaker
  • Unlimited undo and a hint for every move
  • District 1 free. One purchase unlocks the rest. No ads.
