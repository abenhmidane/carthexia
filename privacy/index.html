Privacy

Last updated 17 November 2026

This website

carthexia.com counts visits with GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics service. It sets no cookies and stores no personal data: it records the page visited, the referring site, the browser, the screen size and the country (worked out from the IP address, which is not stored). We use the totals to see which pages people read and which links they follow. Nothing is used for advertising or shared with anyone else.

The Neon Rot web demo

The demo at carthexia.com/neon-rot saves your progress (best scores, last sector, sound settings) in your own browser's local storage. It never leaves your device. Clearing your browser's site data deletes it. The demo is counted by GoatCounter as described above, including taps on its App Store button.

The iPhone app collects no data. Its own policy is here.
